Every part I've designed with came with a datasheet: features up front, absolute maximum ratings, a pinout, and application notes showing how to use it. When I sat down to build a portfolio, that format already answered the questions a reader has about an engineer: what can it do, where are its limits, and how do I connect to it?

## How This Site Is Organized

- **Home** is the front page of the datasheet.
- **Projects** are application notes, one per project.
- **Blog** posts, like this one, are technical notes.
- **About** holds the full specifications.
