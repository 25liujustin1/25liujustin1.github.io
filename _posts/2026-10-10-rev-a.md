Every part I've designed with came with a datasheet: features up front, absolute maximum ratings, a pinout, and a revision history. When I sat down to write an About page, that format already answered the questions a reader has about an engineer: what can it do, where are its limits, and how do I connect to it?

So my [About page](/about/) is the datasheet for the JRL-2029. The rest of the site is a normal portfolio:

- **Projects** has a page for each thing I've built.
- **Blog** is for build logs and debugging stories, like this one.
